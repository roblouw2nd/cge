The process

How it's built.

Same rhythm every engagement — clear phases, weekly demos, written deliverables. Nothing is "in review" forever.

01
Audit (Month 1)

Account-level teardown across every channel. Find the waste, the wins, and the gaps. Restructure the account hierarchy for scale.

02
Instrument (Month 1—2)

Server-side conversions on every channel. Identity resolution. The plumbing that recovers much of the signal lost to iOS and cookie deprecation.

03
Scale (Month 2—12)

Weekly creative shipping, ongoing budget reallocation, and constant testing. Two-week rolling sprints with clear hypotheses.

04
Compound

Quarterly incrementality reviews. Annual planning. The programme gets smarter every month — that's the point of doing it for a year.

Common questions

Before you ask.

What's the minimum monthly spend?+

I'm most useful from $30k/mo upward — below that, the operator overhead doesn't make sense. If you're smaller, I can point you to better-suited specialists.

Do you work with our existing agency?+

Sometimes. Often the cleanest version is replacing them. But if you have a creative shop or platform-specialist you love, I orchestrate around them.

What happens after twelve months?+

Most clients renew. A few graduate to running it in-house with my fractional support. The handover docs make either path workable.
