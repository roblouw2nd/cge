Option C

Fractional growth engineer

—
per year, at your settings
  • Senior operator, hands on keyboard
  • Ads + tracking + web + data — one person, no handoffs
  • Starts in days, not months
  • Deliverable: a system your team keeps

Fair caveats

This compares cost, not value — a great agency beats a mediocre fractional, and a full-time hire wins once there's 40+ hours a week of proven playbook to run. Fractional fits the stage before that: when the playbook still needs building and the work is senior, varied and part-time by nature. If the calculator tells you full-time is right for you, hire full-time.
