The process

How it's built.

Same rhythm every engagement — clear phases, weekly demos, written deliverables. Nothing is "in review" forever.

01
Teardown

Heuristic + data-driven audit of the funnel. Where are users dropping, why, what does the analytics actually say. Hypothesis ranked by expected impact.

02
Set up

Experimentation platform configured. Stats engine validated. Tracking events in place. The team learns the protocol.

03
Ship

Two-week cycles. One winning test per cycle is the target. Win, lose, or learn — every test gets a writeup.

04
Compound

The biggest gains come from the winners getting shipped to production and the losing variants killed quickly. We measure the cumulative lift, not individual tests.

Common questions

Before you ask.

How much traffic do we need?+

For binary conversion tests, you want ~1,000 conversions/week minimum. For lower-volume pages we use Bayesian methods + qualitative research instead.

What if our tests don't win?+

Half of mine don't. That's why we ship in cycles. The winners pay for the losers, and the losers teach you about your users. Bad sign is when nothing moves — that means you need bigger swings.

Do you do redesigns?+

Yes — pages with clear conversion goals get redesigned, not just A/B-tested. Full-page rewrites against fresh hypotheses, then validated.
