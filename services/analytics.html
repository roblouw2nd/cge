The process

How it's built.

Same rhythm every engagement — clear phases, weekly demos, written deliverables. Nothing is "in review" forever.

01
Pull

Connect to your warehouse. Pull the quarter's data. Run the standard battery — funnels, retention, attribution, SEO.

02
Interrogate

Don't just report — interrogate. Why did retention dip in cohort X? What does the SEO traffic correlate with? Where's the lift hiding?

03
Write

A short, opinionated memo. What I'd do next, ranked. No dashboards. No 60-slide decks. A 5-page document that points at decisions.

04
Workshop

A 90-minute working session with your leadership team. Pick the next quarter's priorities. Then I disappear for 12 weeks.

Common questions

Before you ask.

Do we get a dashboard?+

You get a memo. Dashboards are great for monitoring, terrible for decisions. If you need dashboards, the Tracking service builds those — Analytics is for the strategic layer above them.

Can you train our analyst?+

Yes — I often pair with internal analyst hires. The quarterly cycle becomes a coaching loop as they learn to do it without me.

What about real-time?+

You probably don't need real-time. Quarterly is right for strategy. Weekly is right for ops. Hourly is right for fraud detection. Pick the cadence to the question.
