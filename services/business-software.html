The process

How it's built.

Same rhythm every engagement — clear phases, weekly demos, written deliverables. Nothing is "in review" forever.

01
Workflow audit

Shadow your team for a week. Find the manual steps, the spreadsheets passed by email, the Slack messages that should be tickets. Prioritise by hours-saved.

02
Prototype

Sketch the tool in Retool by week two. Real data, real workflows. Your team uses it before it's "done" — feedback is fast.

03
Productionise

Auth, audit logs, error handling, the unsexy stuff. Either stay in Retool or graduate to bespoke code if the tool justifies it.

04
Handover

Your team — or a friendly engineer — owns it. I document everything. Optional ongoing maintenance arrangement.

Common questions

Before you ask.

Retool vs. bespoke — which?+

Default to Retool. Move to bespoke when the tool is customer-facing, performance-critical, or going to outlive Retool's pricing curve.

Will we be locked into Retool?+

No more than you're locked into any SaaS. Most Retool apps can be rewritten in a week of React work if you need to leave.

Can you replace our CRM?+

I'd rather not. CRMs are calcified for good reason. I build the tools that surround your CRM — better workflows on top, better dashboards underneath.
