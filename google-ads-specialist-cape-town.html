What you get

Why a Cape Town specialist works for South African businesses

Same time zone, same market. South African buyers often enquire by WhatsApp or phone before they'll fill in a form. That changes what should count as a conversion and how an account should be built, and it's easy to miss from another market.

International standard, local rate. The same work I do for clients paying in dollars, pounds and dirhams, done from Cape Town.

Who this suits, and who it doesn't

A good fit: you're already spending on Google Ads and suspect a meaningful share is wasted. Leads arrive but quality is poor. You don't trust the conversion numbers. You want one senior person accountable.

Not a fit: you need high-volume social content or brand creative (an agency does that better), or your monthly ad budget is very small, in which case a once-off setup and audit is a better buy than ongoing management. I've written the full list in when not to hire me.
