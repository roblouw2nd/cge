§ 10 — FAQ

Questions
founders ask.

If yours isn't here, the fastest answer is a 30-minute call. Book one.

What is a fractional growth engineer?+

A senior operator who builds a company's growth systems hands-on — paid acquisition, conversion tracking, web, and the data behind them — on a part-time or project basis, then hands the working system to your team. Unlike a fractional CMO, who leads strategy and manages people, a fractional growth engineer ships: campaigns, tracking, integrations, stores, quote platforms.

Fractional growth engineer vs agency — why not just hire an agency?+

An agency gives you an account manager and a shared pod. A fractional growth engineer gives you one senior person who builds and owns the whole system — ads, tracking, web, data — with no handoffs and no markup on juniors. My longest client relationship is five years; that doesn't happen if the work doesn't compound.

Are you actually one person?+

Effectively, yes — CGE is me, Rob. I take a small number of concurrent engagements and run every part personally. For specialist work (design, copy, dev) I bring in trusted collaborators on a per-project basis. You'll always know exactly who you're working with.

How long are your engagements?+

It depends on the service. An audit is 2–4 weeks. A full engine build is six months. A PPC programme typically runs twelve. A CRO sprint is 8–12 weeks. Fractional CGE work is ongoing. Pick the shape that fits the work, not the other way round.

What if it's not working?+

Every engagement carries a no-fault out-clause at the first natural checkpoint — 30 days for sprints, 60 days for builds, 90 days for year-long programmes. Bad work billed in full is worse for both of us. I'd rather you walk.

Who do you work best with?+

Growth-stage businesses with real demand that needs building properly. Current and recent clients span self-storage, luxury transport, hospitality, medical and B2B ecommerce — across the US, UK & Ireland and the Middle East. If you want senior, hands-on help instead of a big agency, that's the fit.

Where are you based, and which hours?+

Cape Town, South Africa — GMT+2. I work flexibly across US and Asian hours and have run engagements across Europe, the Middle East and South Africa. Remote by default, on-site when it earns its travel.

Will you sign an NDA?+

Happily. Send yours over. I keep client identities confidential by default and only feature work publicly with written permission.

How do you handle handover?+

The handover is the deliverable. By the final fortnight of any build, your team will have written runbooks for every system, pair-programmed sessions on the live tooling, and a 60-day check-in scheduled. I don't walk away — I just stop charging.
